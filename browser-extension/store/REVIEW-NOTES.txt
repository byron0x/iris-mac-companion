IRIS browser companion 0.2.0 update

Adds a local crypto-phishing link check, an optional last-7-days history check, an opt-in extension-change badge, and simpler extension review. Required permissions: management, storage, declarativeNetRequest and alarms. Optional webNavigation enables warning pages and checking already-open tabs after consent. History is optional and requested only by the user's Check my last 7 days click. No broad host permissions, page content access, transaction inspection, native messaging or remote code. Known-scam blocking uses local declarative rules; validated domain data updates daily from the fixed ScamSniffer GitHub feed.

Test: open the extension review, paste https://example.com and choose Check link. This returns no exact match, explicitly not a safety verdict. The packaged ScamSniffer GPLv3 hostname snapshot and its date, seven-day delay and exact-match limitations are disclosed. No entered links leave the browser.

Enable Watch for extension changes. The toolbar badge shows how many extensions need review. Keep a recognized extension or turn one off; undo remains available. This is a permission review, not malware detection.

History is processed locally on demand, at most 5,000 entries from seven days. Only matched hostnames are retained locally. Saved checks expire after seven days and are pruned when the extension runs. Forget check & remove history access removes the result and optional permission. No automatic history collection or history deletion.

Connect to IRIS shares the extension report, monitoring state and recent-check aggregate counts directly with app.undercoveriris.io in the same browser for 30 days. Live scam warnings share matched hostname, timestamp and blocked/already-open status after the protection toggle explains this. Matched history-check hostnames and raw URLs are not shared; no scan data is uploaded to the IRIS server. A Google login is not needed to test this browser connection.
